Transparency

What’s not included

We keep the pricing clean. These are the common external costs to be aware of.

Medication costs are separate

GLP-1 medications (e.g., Ozempic/Wegovy/Mounjaro where applicable) are not included in membership pricing. If medication is clinically appropriate, prescriptions and refills follow standard medical practice.

Lab costs may vary

Standard and Premium include a defined bloodwork panel cadence, but the external laboratory fees can vary depending on provider, location, and the specific tests required.

FAQ

Pricing questions

Do you accept insurance?

We do not currently bill insurers directly. If your policy reimburses out-of-network care, we can provide documentation where appropriate — reimbursement depends on your insurer.

What payment methods do you accept?

Typically bank transfer and card payments (where available). Exact options may vary by programme and will be confirmed during your consultation.

How does cancellation work for Standard and Premium?

Standard and Premium are month-to-month. You can cancel anytime. Cancellation takes effect at the end of your current billing period.

Are there refunds?

As a rule, we do not offer refunds for months already in progress, because clinical time and programme capacity are reserved. If there is a billing mistake, we’ll correct it immediately.

Why is The Private Office a 6-month minimum?

Because it is not just more access — it is a standing clinical relationship with priority scheduling and coordination. The minimum ensures continuity and clinical quality.
