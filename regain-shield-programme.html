Why This Exists

In clinical trials, approximately two-thirds of people who stop GLP-1 medication regain most of their weight within 12 months.

Real-world data tells a more nuanced story: Epic Research (2025) found that 56% of semaglutide patients maintained or continued to lose weight at 24 months — with appropriate support. Biology is the baseline challenge.

When semaglutide or tirzepatide leave your system, your appetite hormones surge, your metabolism slows, and your body fights to return to where it was. What you do in that window matters enormously.

The 1 in 3 who keep the weight off don't have superhuman discipline. They have a plan — and someone watching over them.

Regain Shield is built on protocols developed at Élan Clinic Tallinn. It gives you physician oversight, personalised monthly guidance, and an early-warning system — at a fraction of the cost of ongoing consultations.

Is This for You?

Who this is and isn't for.

This is for you if:

  • You recently stopped or are tapering off Ozempic, Wegovy, Saxenda, or Mounjaro
  • You've lost meaningful weight and want to protect it
  • You're not ready for (or don't need) full monthly medical consultations
  • You want physician oversight without clinic pricing

This is NOT for you if:

  • You're currently on GLP-1 medication who still needs dose management → see full Élan Clinic consultation
