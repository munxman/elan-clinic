Mission

Built for the hardest part of weight management — keeping the results.

GLP-1 medications helped millions lose weight. But most clinics stop supporting patients right when the biology gets difficult: during maintenance. When medication is reduced or stopped, appetite signals rebound, energy expenditure adapts, and old patterns return.

Élan Clinic was founded for one reason: there is no clinic focused specifically on post-GLP-1 maintenance. We built a physician-led protocol for the transition phase — with monitoring, structure, and clear decision rules — so patients do not have to fight regain alone.
