Last updated: 2026-03-03

Élan Clinic is a licensed healthcare provider. We handle personal data in accordance with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Health data is treated as special category data (GDPR Article 9) and is processed only with a lawful basis and, where required, explicit consent.

What we collect

  • Health data only when you become a patient and within the scope of providing healthcare services.
  • Basic technical data required for website delivery and security (e.g., server logs).

Purpose & legal basis

  • Providing healthcare services and communicating with patients.
  • Responding to enquiries and appointment requests.
  • Compliance with legal obligations applicable to healthcare providers.
  • Ensuring website security and preventing abuse.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may use EU-based service providers (processors) strictly to deliver the service (e.g., email, scheduling). Where possible, we keep data in EU/EEA systems.

Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary for the purposes above and as required by law for healthcare documentation.

Your rights

Depending on the context and applicable law, you may have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, and to data portability. For health records, certain rights may be limited by medical record-keeping obligations.
