Testimonial consent (GDPR)

Last updated: 2026-03-03

Élan Clinic publishes patient testimonials only with explicit written informed consent. Health data is special category data under GDPR Article 9.

What this means

  • We will not publish a testimonial without documented consent on file.
  • You can choose how you are identified (first name only, initials, or anonymised).
  • You can withdraw consent at any time. We will remove the testimonial promptly (target: within 72 hours).

How to give or withdraw consent

Please contact us at info@elan.clinic and we will provide the consent form and instructions.

Nõusolek tagasiside avaldamiseks (ET)

Avaldame inimeste tagasisidet ainult kirjaliku informeeritud nõusoleku alusel (GDPR art 9). Nõusoleku saab igal ajal tagasi võtta; eemaldame tagasiside esimesel võimalusel (eesmärk: 72 tunni jooksul). Küsi nõusoleku vormi: info@elan.clinic.
